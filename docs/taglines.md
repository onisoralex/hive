# Hive Foundry — Taglines

## Selected

**"Human mind. Hive hands."** — parallel structure, clear division of roles, punchy.

## Alternatives

| Tagline | Notes |
|---|---|
| "Built by human and hive." | First proposal. Honest and direct. Also strong. |
| "Cast by human and hive." | "Cast" is a foundry term — thematically tighter than "built." |
| "Small tools. Bigger things." | Speaks to the growth philosophy. Less identity, more mission. |
| "The hive builds. You direct." | More explanatory, slightly less evocative. |
| "Forged together." | Minimal. Risks being too vague. |
